# Rahala roadmap

## Phase 2 baseline

The v0.2.0 catalog spans 195 countries and 116 missions. The offline atlas and encyclopedia cover all supported countries, and the adventure supports flags, capitals, map selection, and country silhouettes.

## Adventure expansion

- Expand to 250 progressive levels across the eight chapter paths. Author route objectives, balanced question mixes, and cumulative world challenges. Keep published question IDs and level IDs attached to their existing content.
- Reach all eight game modes by adding landmarks, geography puzzles, physical geography, and optional speed challenges. Keep learning missions untimed and add a non-timed alternative for speed-focused practice.
- Source and license landmark photos and illustrations. Add validated natural geography for oceans, rivers, deserts, and mountain ranges, including Antarctica. Track provenance and regional conventions for every new item.
- Build drag-and-drop map puzzles using the atlas shapes. Include a tap-to-place alternative and test targets for small territories, multiple islands, and enlarged text.

## Retention and presentation

- Add rotating daily challenges that work offline and keep first-mastery XP idempotent.
- Introduce achievements and local player statistics for skill mastery, chapter completion, review habits, and passport coverage.
- Expand original atlas artwork, premium animations, and sound effects. Tune frame times on low-end phones and preserve the reduced-motion and sound preferences.

## Android release preparation

- Establish an owner-controlled signing identity and keep signing material out of source control. Test a same-certificate update with existing saves and prepare an offline transfer path for future preview builds.
- Test a signed release build on physical phones, including airplane mode, screen readers, large text, cold start, install, and upgrade.
- Prepare Play Store Arabic assets, content rating, privacy and data safety disclosures, Android App Bundle checks, and a closed-testing rollout for owner review.
